About the portal

Better connections for Vanua Levu yaqona

A simple directory that helps farmers, buyers and exporters discover each other and start a conversation.

Our purpose

The Labasa Yaqona Portal is designed to make information about available yaqona easier to find across Labasa, Cakaudrove and the wider Vanua Levu region. Approved farmers can post listings, while buyers and exporters can search and contact them.

What the portal does not do

  • It does not process or hold payments.
  • It does not certify quality or guarantee that a listing is accurate.
  • It does not replace traditional trading relationships or the Fiji Kava Standard.
  • It does not provide export paperwork or regulatory approval.

Contact

For help with an account or listing, contact the portal administrator through the organisation operating this website.

Region served
Labasa, Macuata, Cakaudrove, Bua and the wider Vanua Levu area, Fiji.

Safety reminder

Confirm identity, quantity, quality, collection arrangements and payment terms directly with the other party before trading.
